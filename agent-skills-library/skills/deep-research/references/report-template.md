1. Quick answer

## 1. Quick answer (single fact or small question)
```
<Direct answer in one to three sentences, with the date it is valid for if time-sensitive.>
<One line of context or caveat if it matters.>
Source: <publisher, title, date, link>
```

## 2. Research brief (1 to 2 pages)
```
# <Topic>: research brief
Date: <YYYY-MM-DD>   Prepared for: <audience>   Confidence: High/Medium/Low

## Bottom line
2 to 5 sentences: the answer, the recommendation, or the state of knowledge.

## Key findings
1. <Finding stated as a full sentence.> Evidence: <fact, number, date> [S1][S2].
2. ...
3. ...

## What is uncertain or disputed
- <Claim> : Source A says ..., Source B says ...; likely reason for the difference: ...

## Implications / next steps
- <Action or decision guidance>

## Sources
[S1] <Author/Org>, "<Title>", <Publisher>, <Date>. <URL>
[S2] ...
```

## 5. Literature review outline
```
1. Introduction: research question, scope, motivation
2. Method: databases, search strings, inclusion/exclusion, screening flow, quality appraisal
3. Background: key concepts and definitions
4. Thematic synthesis: themes/approaches; for each: representative studies, findings, agreement/conflict, quality
5. Comparison table: study | year | data/setting | method | main result | limitations
6. Gaps and open problems; research opportunities
7. Conclusion
8. References (consistent style)
```
Extraction matrix columns: citation, research question, method, dataset, metrics, results, limitations, relevance to my question, quality notes.

## 6. Style rules
- Lead with the answer; support after. Use the reader's vocabulary.
- One claim per sentence where possible; put the source right after the claim.
- Use tables for comparisons and numeric data; avoid decorative formatting.
- Distinguish fact, inference, and opinion in wording ("The documentation states...", "This suggests...", "The vendor claims...").
- Include dates for anything time-sensitive; avoid "recently" and "currently" without a date.
- Keep quotations under about 15 words, attributed; paraphrase the rest.
- State confidence and limitations plainly; do not bury them.
